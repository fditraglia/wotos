Reveal the answer

The tempting answer is 1/2: the card is either Obama/Obama or Obama/Gaga, so it seems to be a coin flip between them. But seeing Obama is not equally likely for those two cards. Count faces instead of cards.

Gaga/Gaga
Obama/Gaga other side Gaga
Obama/Obama other side Obama other side Obama

Each of the six faces is equally likely to be the one facing up. Three of them are Obama, and two of those three belong to the Obama/Obama card. So the probability that Obama is on the other side is 2/3.

In symbols, let F be the event that Obama is facing up and B the event that he is on the other side. Then

P(B | F) = P(B ∩ F)P(F) = 1/31/2 = 2/3.
